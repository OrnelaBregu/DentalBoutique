# **📘 DentalBoutique**

# **Company Workflows & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP Manual)**

**Operational Version 1.0**

This document outlines detailed step-by-step workflows for all major operational functions at **DentalBoutique**. These workflows are mandatory for all locations.

---

# **1️⃣ Front Desk & Reception Workflows**

---

## **1.1 Incoming Call Handling Workflow**

**Target Answer Time:** ≤ 3 rings  
 **Call Duration Goal:** 3–6 minutes

### **Step 1: Greeting (Within 5 seconds)**

“Good morning, thank you for calling DentalBoutique, this is \[Name\], how may I help you today?”

### **Step 2: Identify Caller Type**

* New Patient

* Existing Patient

* Emergency

* Insurance/Billing

* Vendor

  ### **Step 3: Verify Information (Existing Patients)**

* Full name

* Date of birth

* Phone number

* Confirm last visit date

  ### **Step 4: Booking Appointment**

* Offer 2 time slots minimum

* Confirm procedure type

* Confirm insurance coverage

* Review cancellation policy

* Collect email for confirmation

  ### **Step 5: Closing**

“You are booked for \[Date & Time\]. We will send you a confirmation. Please arrive 10 minutes early.”

---

## **1.2 Missed Call Protocol**

* Return call within 30 minutes

* Log attempt in CRM

* Attempt 2 follow-ups within 24 hours

  ---

  ## **1.3 New Patient Intake Workflow**

**Time Allocation:** 10–15 minutes

1. Send digital intake form immediately after booking

2. Confirm completion 24 hours prior

3. Verify insurance eligibility 48 hours prior

4. Collect photo ID and insurance card at check-in

5. Create EHR profile before seating patient

   ---

   ## **1.4 Appointment Confirmation Workflow**

* Automated SMS: 72 hours prior

* SMS reminder: 24 hours prior

* Manual call if no response by 12 hours prior

* Reschedule if unconfirmed by 4 hours prior

  ---

  # **2️⃣ Patient Check-In Workflow**

**Target Check-In Time:** 3–5 minutes

1. Greet patient by name

2. Confirm appointment type

3. Confirm medical history updates

4. Collect co-pay (if applicable)

5. Seat patient within 10 minutes of arrival

   ---

   # **3️⃣ Clinical Room Turnover Workflow**

**Target Turnover Time:** 8–12 minutes

1. Remove disposable items

2. Transport instruments to sterilization

3. Disinfect surfaces (chair, light, tray, counter)

4. Replace barriers

5. Restock tray per procedure setup checklist

6. Confirm room readiness in system

   ---

   # **4️⃣ Sterilization Workflow**

   ## **4.1 Instrument Processing Steps**

1. Pre-soak in enzymatic cleaner (10 minutes)

2. Ultrasonic cleaning (10 minutes)

3. Rinse & dry

4. Package & seal

5. Autoclave:

   * 132°C (270°F) for 4 minutes

6. Cool-down cycle (30 minutes)

7. Log batch number

8. Store in sterile cabinet

**Spore Testing:** Weekly  
 **Failure Protocol:** Immediate re-sterilization \+ equipment service call

---

# **5️⃣ Digital X-Ray Workflow**

## **5.1 Bitewing X-Ray Procedure**

1. Confirm patient identity

2. Apply lead apron \+ thyroid collar

3. Position sensor

4. Align X-ray head

5. Step behind shield

6. Capture image

7. Review clarity immediately

8. Retake if distorted

**Average Exposure Time:** 0.1–0.3 seconds  
 **Target Retake Rate:** \< 5%

---

# **6️⃣ Intraoral Scanner Workflow (e.g., Crown Prep)**

**Time Allocation:** 5–8 minutes

1. Dry field isolation

2. Apply scanning powder if needed

3. Scan upper arch

4. Scan lower arch

5. Capture bite scan

6. Review digital model for voids

7. Send STL file to lab within 15 minutes

8. Document scan completion

   ---

   # **7️⃣ CBCT Scan Workflow**

1. Verify prescription & indication

2. Remove metal objects

3. Position patient head stable

4. Select scan field size (FOV)

5. Confirm radiation parameters

6. Capture scan

7. Review for artifacts

8. Save DICOM file

9. Upload to secure system

**Scan Time:** 20–40 seconds  
 **Processing Time:** 2–5 minutes

---

# **8️⃣ Treatment Planning Workflow**

1. Clinical exam

2. X-ray review

3. Periodontal charting

4. Diagnosis coding

5. Present 2–3 treatment options

6. Provide printed cost estimate

7. Document acceptance or deferral

8. Schedule next appointment before patient leaves

**Target Treatment Acceptance:** 70%

---

# **9️⃣ Insurance Processing Workflow**

1. Verify eligibility (48 hours prior)

2. Confirm coverage percentages

3. Submit pre-authorization if required

4. Submit claim within 24 hours post-treatment

5. Track outstanding claims weekly

6. Escalate unpaid claims after 30 days

   ---

   # **🔟 Billing & Payment Workflow**

1. Generate invoice immediately after treatment

2. Review charges with patient

3. Collect co-pay same day

4. Offer financing options if needed

5. Provide receipt

6. Log payment in accounting system

**Same-Day Collection Target:** 95%

---

# **11️⃣ Emergency Patient Workflow**

**Goal:** Seat within 30 minutes

1. Identify pain severity (1–10 scale)

2. Prioritize bleeding/swelling

3. Notify dentist immediately

4. Take X-rays within 10 minutes

5. Provide temporary relief treatment

6. Schedule follow-up before discharge

   ---

   # **12️⃣ Hygiene Recall Workflow**

1. Auto-generate recall list weekly

2. Contact patients due within 30 days

3. SMS \+ email \+ phone attempt

4. Track response rate

5. Escalate after 3 failed attempts

**Reappointment Rate Target:** ≥ 85%

---

# **13️⃣ End-of-Day Closing Workflow**

1. Confirm all charts completed

2. Balance payments vs POS

3. Run daily revenue report

4. Backup system check

5. Turn off equipment

6. Lock sterilization area

7. Secure patient files

8. Activate alarm system

**Closing Duration:** 20–30 minutes

---

# **14️⃣ Opening Workflow**

1. Arrive 30 minutes before first patient

2. Power on all equipment

3. Sterilization log check

4. Print daily schedule

5. Confirm staff attendance

6. Check emergency kit

7. Confirm rooms stocked

   ---

   # **15️⃣ Complaint Handling Workflow**

1. Listen fully without interruption

2. Acknowledge concern

3. Document incident

4. Escalate to Practice Manager within 24 hrs

5. Provide resolution within 72 hrs

6. Follow-up call after resolution

   ---

   # **16️⃣ Marketing Lead Conversion Workflow**

1. Respond to website inquiries within 15 minutes

2. Call within 30 minutes

3. Offer free consultation

4. Book within 48 hours

5. Track conversion rate

**Lead-to-Patient Target:** 35–50%
